no flowers please

no flowers please, no mourning parade
don't honor the spot where my bones have been laid
honor the room where i laughed and cried
where i stumble through life, not just where i died

don't leave roses at my marble cold stone
but warm up the hearts of those i left alone
lay lilies beside my parents tired hearts
bring stories and sunlight, not silence and dread

leave my ashes let the wind take them wide
but please sit with my people when their sorrow won't hide
hold close to the hearts i used to know
be there when their tears begin to flow

don't stand by my grave with tears and a sigh
go sit with my friends let the laughter run high
see to my children don't bring me bouquets
hold their small hands through the loneliest days

should have noticed the death whilst my living days
my silent goodbyes in hundred different ways
so if u must grieve then grieve with intent
make joy from time together well spent

and stop talking about taking your own life
taking a life, what a weird thing to say
who does it belong to? who takes it away?
once its over its not you who sheds the tear,
its others who suffer from when you disappear

And when your own sunset comes into view,
I hope you’ll remember what I asked of you.
No flowers please, no sorrow, nor dread
Just live for the living, and love me instead.
